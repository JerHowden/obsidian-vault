## Participation

Participation within the discussion threads will be measured and assessed. Appropriate discussion responses should:

- be between 100-300 words in length.
- demonstrate a deep understanding of the content ideas, providing appropriate reference to content in the course as well as outside resources.
- address all pieces of the prompt clearly and thoroughly.
- include personal connections with topic content.
- include evidence of interaction with other participants in the discussion threads in a timely manner throughout the course period.
- be proofread for editing, grammar, and spelling errors.
- be appropriate, connected, relevant, and informed.
- be written with a respectful tone and proper netiquette
